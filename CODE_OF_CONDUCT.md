# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of LOREKEEP, pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and scope

LOREKEEP maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

This Code applies in LOREKEEP project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project in public. Representation includes using an official project account or acting as an appointed representative online or at an event.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the [LOREKEEP organization owners](https://github.com/orgs/lorekeep-pwa/people) or through GitHub's private contact options. Do not report sensitive incidents in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, and will respect the reporter's privacy and security.

Maintainers may use the following response, taking the circumstances and impact into account:

1. **Correction:** A private written warning that explains the issue and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** A warning with clear conditions for continued participation. This may include a period of no contact with the people involved or enforcing the Code.
3. **Temporary ban:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication in project spaces. Attempts to evade it may result in a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban:** A permanent ban from public interaction in project spaces for a sustained pattern of violations, harassment, or serious aggression.
